The team

The institute's clinical front door

The Students Wellness Centre (SWC) is the institute's clinical front door. A dedicated team of mental-health professionals offers confidential one-to-one counselling, group sessions and crisis support to every student.

Psychiatric care, any hour

With a psychiatrist available at the campus hospital round the clock, no student in acute distress has to wait until morning or travel off campus for specialist care.

  • Available 24/7 at the IIT Bombay Hospital
  • No travelling off campus for specialist care
  • Part of the care stage of the continuum, alongside SWC and empanelled hospitals
Beyond the clinic

Also offered by SWC

More at swc.iitb.ac.in

Care reaches students where they live and learn, and the people around them are trained to notice when someone needs help.

  • Gatekeeper training for hostel wardens and security staff
  • Workshops for faculty
  • Counselling sessions held in hostels
  • Group sessions and mindfulness workshops
  • Peer-support networks for students
  • Wellbeing events and campus outreach
What's next for care

More counsellors, longer hours, and a counsellor for every hostel and department

Counselling until 10 pm on weekdays and as a full service on weekends, with a familiar contact counsellor close to every student.
