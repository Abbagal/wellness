Talk to someone who listens.

A space to talk

Openly, without pressure or judgement. Every student who walks into the Student Wellness Centre is heard, respected and supported.

Counsellors listen with empathy, respect your privacy, help you explore other perspectives and support you in making your own informed decisions. When it helps, they connect you with further resources, and trained psychometricians can carry out assessments that point to the right kind of support.

The Student Wellness Centre began in 1987 and is one of the oldest student counselling centres in higher education.

You do not need to be in crisis
  • Stress and anxiety
  • Low mood
  • Academic pressure
  • Relationships
  • Settling in
  • Understanding yourself better

What to expect

However you arrive, this is what you can count on.

A safe, respectful space

A setting where you can speak freely and be understood.

Qualified counsellors

Trained mental health professionals who listen without judgement.

Strict confidentiality

What you share stays private.

At your own pace

Support shaped around what you need, for a single concern or simply to talk.

Assessments, when useful

Psychometric assessments that help identify specific concerns and guide care.

More support if needed

Referral to hospitals and counselling centres in the city through external counselling.

How to book

Choose whichever feels easiest.

  1. 1

    Book online

    Open the SWC appointment portal and pick a slot. It works only on the IITB network, not on mobile data or off campus.

  2. 2

    Or write to us

    Email wellness@iitb.ac.in with your name, roll number, contact number and preferred email address.

  3. 3

    Or just walk in

    Come to the Student Wellness Centre during walk-in hours, any day of the week.

  4. 4

    Prefer a centre in the city?

    External counselling offers in-person sessions at hospitals and counselling centres in Mumbai. Book through my.iitb.ac.in/swc-ext on the IITB network, or email incharge.swc@iitb.ac.in with the subject “External Counselling Required”.

If it cannot wait for an appointment, the 24x7 Wellness Response Team is a call away: +91 8356-810252.
