Wellbeing Quick Response Team

One number. One trained team. Any crisis, any hour.

The Wellbeing Quick Response Team (WQRT) will be a dedicated, round-the-clock team that handles every crisis situation on campus. Instead of a response that depends on whoever happens to be reachable, every emergency will be met by the same trained, accountable team.

How a crisis will be handled

  1. STEP 1Alert

    Anyone calls the WQRT number

  2. STEP 2Dispatch

    Team leaves in the dedicated vehicle

  3. STEP 3On site

  4. STEP 4Clinical

Part C · Future targets · 2

RISE IITB: Wellness App and Universal Screening

Resilience · Inner strength · Self-care · Emotional well-being

RISE IITB will bring every wellness service onto one platform, with one login, and make routine wellbeing screening as normal as a health check-up. Every student will be screened, and every result will be followed up by a person.

Universal screening: four categories, every result followed up

At admissionall new students
Every semesterall students, validated tools

Categories are seen only by SWC clinicians and the Student Wellness office. They are never shown to students as labels and never shared with faculty.

Safeguards

  • Hosted on IIT Bombay servers; owned by IIT Bombay
  • Results seen only by authorised clinicians
  • Never part of academic, placement or disciplinary records
  • AI for self-help only; people make every decision

Roll-out in six months

AlphaPilot: one hostel, one department
BetaInstitute-wide
Full releaseAll features live

Developed through an open tender

More Counsellors, Longer Hours

Help when students are free to seek it: in the evenings and at weekends

Students attend classes and labs through the day, and many reach out only once the day is over. We will increase the number of counsellors so that counselling is available until 10 pm on weekdays and as a full service on weekends, not only for walk-ins.

  • More counsellors: enough to staff evenings and weekends without stretching the day team
  • No class missed: students can seek help without missing lectures or labs
  • Scheduled weekends: appointments on Saturdays and Sundays, not only walk-ins

Part C · Future targets · 4

Counsellor Assigned for Every Hostel and Department

Support that is local, familiar and easy to reach

Each hostel and department will have one SWC counsellor as its contact person. Wardens, Heads of Department, faculty advisors, mentors and ambassadors will always know exactly whom to call, and students will see a familiar face.

What the assigned counsellor does

  • Regular, visible presence in the hostel or department
  • First call for wardens, HoDs and faculty advisors
  • Works with the wellbeing ambassadors and mentors
  • Links local concerns to SHINE and the WQRT

Why it matters

  • Familiarity lowers the barrier to seeking help
  • Early concerns are picked up close to the student
  • Clear accountability for every hostel and department
  • Better coordination across the support network

Administrative Support for Wellness

A dedicated leadership and administrative backbone for a growing mission

Student wellness at IIT Bombay now spans round-the-clock services, a new response team, a wellness app, institute-wide screening and a larger counselling team. To run this reliably, two dedicated positions must be created: a Chief Wellness Officer and a Deputy Registrar for Student Wellness.
