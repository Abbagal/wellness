Overview

The Student Wellness Ecosystem

One office, five units, one continuum of care

In September 2026, IIT Bombay took the initiative of creating a new post: Associate Dean, Student Wellness. This office has been created specifically for the overall wellbeing of students. It brings five units together under one roof, so that prevention, support and care work as one coordinated system.

SHINE Committee

Support, Help and Intervention for Nurturing Empowerment

Some students go through periods when they cannot cope alone: a mental-health condition, the loss of a parent or loved one, a long illness, a family crisis, or a slide into academic difficulty. Too often these students withdraw quietly, fall behind, and are noticed only when the situation has become serious. SHINE makes sure that every such student is identified early, receives coordinated care, and is given a genuine opportunity to recover and complete their degree.

Who SHINE is for

Mental-health concerns

Depression, anxiety or other conditions affecting daily life and studies

Personal loss

Bereavement, family crises, serious illness or extended absence

Academic distress

Multiple backlogs or a sharp decline in grades

Addiction and behaviour

Substance use, digital addiction or behavioural concerns

Disengagement

Missing classes, or not registering for a semester

Referral by others

Concerns raised by friends, mentors, faculty or wardens

How it works

How SHINE helps the student

One case owner

A single person coordinates counselling, academics and advising, so the student does not have to navigate the system alone

Care that is connected

Counselling and psychiatric care are linked to the student's academic plan

Room to recover

An individual action plan, with academic flexibility and policy support where needed

Confidential and followed up

Handled with discretion and reviewed until the student is back on track

Part B · Ongoing plans Restructuring

Peer Circles with Faculty Advisors

Restructuring mentorship for effective faculty–mentor–mentee coordination

Faculty advising and student mentorship already exist at IIT Bombay, but they often run in parallel, with little day-to-day contact between the faculty member and the student mentors. The restructuring brings them into one coordinated unit: each Faculty Advisor works directly with a small team of student mentors, and each mentor anchors a peer circle of mentees. Every student is known by name, and a concern raised anywhere in the circle reaches the right person quickly.

How coordination will work

  1. Menteeshares a concern
  2. Student mentorlistens, informs the FA
  3. Faculty Advisormeets the student
  4. Counsellor / SHINEwhen more help is needed

Student mentor Mentee · FA: 20–30 students across three circles

Clear structure

Each Faculty Advisor is formally linked to three mentors and their circles

Regular contact

Scheduled FA–mentor meetings and mentor–mentee check-ins every semester

Accountability

Mentoring guidelines followed, with student feedback on advisors and mentors

Part C · Future target 1

Wellbeing Quick Response Team

One number. One trained team. Any crisis, any hour.

The Wellbeing Quick Response Team (WQRT) will be a dedicated, round-the-clock team that handles every crisis situation on campus. Instead of a response that depends on whoever happens to be reachable, every emergency will be met by the same trained, accountable team.

Dedicated vehicle
WQRT · on duty 24/7
2 Counsellorsone male, one female in rotation
1 Psychiatristalways available on a phone call
2 Security guardsensure safety on site
Dedicated cell phone number

How a crisis will be handled

  1. 1Alert

    Anyone calls the WQRT number

  2. 2Dispatch

    Team leaves in the dedicated vehicle

  3. 3On site

    Counsellors support; security keeps safe

  4. 4Clinical

    Psychiatrist on call; hospital if needed

  5. 5Follow-up

    Handover to SWC and SHINE

Calls can come from the student, a friend, a warden, campus security, a mentor or a wellbeing ambassador.

One number to remember

Publicised in every hostel, department and on the RISE IITB app

Minutes, not hours

A team ready to move at any hour, with its own vehicle

Clear roles

Trained staff, defined protocols, and every case handed over for follow-up

Part C · Future target 2

RISE IITB: Wellness App and Universal Screening

Resilience · Inner strength · Self-care · Emotional well-being

RISE IITB will bring every wellness service onto one platform, with one login, and make routine wellbeing screening as normal as a health check-up. Every student will be screened, and every result will be followed up by a person.

Universal screening: four categories, every result followed up

At admissionall new students
Every semesterall students, validated tools

Categories are seen only by SWC clinicians and the Student Wellness office. They are never shown to students as labels and never shared with faculty.

Safeguards

  • Hosted on IIT Bombay servers; owned by IIT Bombay
  • Results seen only by authorised clinicians
  • Never part of academic, placement or disciplinary records
  • AI for self-help only; people make every decision

Roll-out in six months

AlphaPilot: one hostel, one department
BetaInstitute-wide
Full releaseAll features live

Developed through an open tender

Part C · Future target 4

Counsellor Assigned for Every Hostel and Department

Support that is local, familiar and easy to reach

Each hostel and each department will have one SWC counsellor assigned as its contact person. Wardens, Heads of Department, faculty advisors, mentors and ambassadors will always know exactly whom to call, and students will see a familiar face.

What the assigned counsellor does

  • Regular, visible presence in the hostel or department
  • First call for wardens, HoDs and faculty advisors
  • Works with the wellbeing ambassadors and mentors
  • Links local concerns to SHINE and the WQRT

Why it matters

  • Familiarity lowers the barrier to seeking help
  • Early concerns are picked up close to the student
  • Clear accountability for every hostel and department
  • Better coordination across the support network

Part C · Future target 5

Administrative Support for Wellness

A dedicated leadership and administrative backbone for a growing mission

Student wellness at IIT Bombay now spans round-the-clock services, a new response team, a wellness app, institute-wide screening and a larger counselling team. To run this reliably, two dedicated positions must be created: a Chief Wellness Officer and a Deputy Registrar for Student Wellness.
