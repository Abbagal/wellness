Our Work

No student at IIT Bombay should have to face a hard time alone.

Counselling, a 24x7 response team, psychiatry, peer support and flourishing programmes work together as one system, so that there is always a next step and someone to take it with you.

Prevent

Workshops, mentoring and early support in every hostel and department.

Respond

Counsellors, psychiatry and a response team that reaches you at any hour.

Flourish

Programmes that help students grow and thrive, not just cope.
