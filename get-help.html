Right now

Three ways to get support today

The Students Wellness Centre offers round-the-clock help in a crisis, and a psychiatrist is available at the IIT Bombay Hospital day and night.

  1. If someone is in immediate danger

    Call 112, India's emergency number, and stay with the person until help arrives.

  2. Reach SWC's 24/7 crisis support

    The Students Wellness Centre provides round-the-clock help in a crisis. Current contact details are on swc.iitb.ac.in.

  3. Go to the IIT Bombay Hospital

    A psychiatrist is available round the clock on campus, so no student in acute distress has to wait until morning or travel off campus.

Supporting someone else

Worried about a friend?

Too often, students who are struggling withdraw quietly and are noticed only when things have become serious. You can help someone get support early.

  1. Notice and reach out

    Check in privately. Tell them what you have noticed and that you care.

  2. Listen without judging

    Let them talk. You don't need to have answers or fix the problem.

  3. Connect them to help

    Encourage them to contact SWC. Offer to go with them, or tell a mentor, Faculty Advisor, warden or Wellbeing Ambassador.

  4. Act fast if they are in danger

    Don't leave them alone. Call 112 or take them to the IIT Bombay Hospital.

Signs that someone may need support

Mental-health concerns

Low mood, anxiety or other changes affecting daily life and studies

Personal loss

Bereavement, a family crisis, serious illness or a long absence

Academic distress

Multiple backlogs or a sharp decline in grades

Addiction and behaviour

Substance use, digital addiction or behavioural changes

Disengagement

Missing classes, or not registering for a semester

Raise a concern

Friends, mentors, faculty and wardens can all refer a student to SHINE

Coming next Planned

One number. One trained team. Any crisis, any hour.

The planned Wellbeing Quick Response Team will meet every emergency on campus with the same trained, accountable team: two counsellors, a psychiatrist on call and two security guards, with a dedicated vehicle and one phone number.

How the WQRT will work
  • Calls can come from the student, a friend, a warden, campus security, a mentor or a wellbeing ambassador
  • The number will be publicised in every hostel, department and on the RISE IITB app
  • Every case is handed over to SWC and SHINE for follow-up

In an emergency, don't wait.Call the national emergency number, or Tele-MANAS for free 24/7 mental health support.
