Discover the four pillars of RISE

Resilience

Setbacks are part of IIT life: a tough quiz, a rejection, a hard semester. Resilience is learning to recover, and asking for help along the way.

Inner strength

Your values, your strengths and your own pace. Inner strength grows when you stop measuring yourself only by grades.

Self-care

Sleep, food, movement and a little time away from the screen. Self-care is not a reward for finishing your work; it is what lets you do it.

Emotional well-being

Naming what you feel, sharing it with someone you trust, and knowing that every feeling is welcome here.
