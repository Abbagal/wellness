Our Work

Wellness is not a service we offer. It is a culture we build.

At IIT Bombay, student wellness goes beyond treating distress. We work to prevent it, to nurture each student's strengths, to respond within minutes when it matters, and to walk alongside every student from the first day to graduation day.

Prevent

We build resilience early, through workshops, yoga and mindfulness, student mentors and Wellbeing Ambassadors in every hostel and department.

Respond

Professional counsellors, online sessions, 24x7 psychiatry and a 24x7 Wellness Response Team mean help is within reach at any hour.

Flourish

Through the Flourishing Hub, we help students find meaning, strength and joy in campus life, not just get through it.

We do not wait for students to fall. We build the ground beneath them.
