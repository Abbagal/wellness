When care needs a clinician

Some concerns need clinical care alongside counselling: an assessment, medication, or steady support through a difficult stretch. A psychiatrist is on call around the clock, both for urgent situations and for ongoing care.

Psychiatric care works hand in hand with counselling. Many students see both a counsellor and a psychiatrist.

How to reach it

  1. 1

    If it is urgent

    Call or WhatsApp the 24x7 Wellness Response Team on +91 8356-810252. A psychiatrist on call is part of their response.

  2. 2

    For ongoing care

    Talk to a CARE counsellor about what you are going through; they can help you take the next step.
